**Treat It or Leave It? Malignancy Diagnosis of Skin Lesions** · [Slides (PDF)](https://github.com/tongtz/tongtz.github.io/blob/main/docs/presentation.pdf)
A clinical decision support system that classifies dermoscopic lesion images (ISIC 2016, 900 images) as benign or malignant. It compares a classical SVM baseline against a ResNet50 CNN. *Duke AIPI 540, with Christine Park & Hank Chen.*

**Photo-to-Avatar Image Translation** · [Slides (PDF)](https://github.com/tongtz/photo2avatar/blob/main/presentation.pdf)
Deep generative image-to-image translation that turns real portrait photos into stylized avatars.
